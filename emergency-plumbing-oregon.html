Emergency plumbing

Water emergency? Act safely first.

When a leak or backup is causing damage, take immediate safety precautions and request plumbing assistance. Online requests are reviewed as availability allows.

Burst or leaking pipe

If safe, turn off the nearest shutoff valve or the main water supply. Keep away from electrical outlets and appliances in wet areas. Request professional repair.

Overflowing drains or sewage

Stop using affected fixtures. Avoid contact with contaminated water and keep children and pets out of the area. Seek qualified assistance promptly.

Water heater trouble

For leaks, unusual odors or overheating, follow the equipment manufacturer’s safety instructions. If there is a suspected gas leak, leave the area and contact your gas utility or emergency services.

Emergency response and scheduling

Choose “As soon as possible” when submitting your request. A form submission does not guarantee an immediate visit or 24-hour availability. For immediate danger, call 911.

What to include in your request

Describe what is happening, whether you have shut off the water, any known hazards and the location of the affected fixture. These details help us assess urgency.
