How do I handle a leaking pipe?

When safe, stop the water at the nearest fixture shutoff or the home's main shutoff valve. Avoid electrical hazards and request qualified assistance for a repair.

Can cold weather affect Oregon plumbing?

Yes. Outdoor faucets and exposed pipes can be vulnerable during cold snaps. Insulation and appropriate winterization help reduce the risk of freezing.

Common urgent problems

When plumbing can't wait.

💧

Burst or leaking pipes

Water escaping rapidly from a wall, ceiling or exposed line.

🚽

Sewage & drain backups

Wastewater coming into a toilet, shower, or lower-level drain.

🔥

Water heater leaks

Leaks from the tank or fittings that could damage surrounding areas.

❄

Frozen pipe concerns

Cold snaps can put unprotected plumbing under stress.

First things first

Three safe steps

01
Shut off water, if safe

Locate the nearest fixture valve or your main water shutoff.

02
Stay clear of hazards

Keep away from standing water near electrical equipment. Do not enter unsafe areas.

03
Get appropriate help

Contact qualified assistance immediately for urgent repairs. For gas smell or immediate danger, leave the area and contact emergency services or your utility.
